# Asset Management

Assets are distinct from consumable inventory.

## Models

- `OperationalAsset` — name, code, serial, location, status, purchase date
- `AssetCategory`
- `AssetAssignment` — staff / label assignment history

## Statuses

`active`, `available`, `assigned`, `maintenance`, `damaged`, `retired`

Examples: gym equipment, spa devices, kitchen gear, projectors, event furniture.
